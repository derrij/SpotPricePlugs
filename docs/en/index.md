## How it works

1. **Find plugs** — the app scans your Wi-Fi network and detects Tasmota and Shelly plugs.
2. **Choose hours** — for example, 4 cheapest hours per day, VAT included, never above your price cap.
3. **Done** — every day after Nord Pool prices are published (≈13:00 CET) the schedule updates itself.

## Supported plugs

Any smart plug with **Tasmota** or **Shelly** firmware works. For example:

- [Nous A1T](https://nous.technology/product/a1t.html) — a Tasmota plug (EU, 16 A)
- [Shelly Plug M Gen3](https://www.shelly.com/products/shelly-plug-m-gen3-white) — a Shelly Gen3 plug

They can be bought from the [Shelly official website](https://www.shelly.com/), the
[Nous store](https://nous.technology/) or your local electronics shops.

## Typical savings

A 2 kW water heater that heats 4 kWh per day costs roughly **€200–250/year** at average
prices — but only **€75–110/year** if it heats during the cheapest hours. A single plug
pays for itself in a couple of months. See the full calculation in
[the blog]({{ site.baseurl }}/en/blog/save-on-water-heating/).

## Requirements

- Android 8.0+, same Wi-Fi network as the plugs
- Plug running Tasmota or Shelly firmware (no web-UI password)
- Nord Pool area: LV, EE, LT, FI, SE1–4, DK1–2, NO1–5, PL, DE, AT, BE, NL, FR
